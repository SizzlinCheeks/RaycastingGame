10 points for a hit, 60 for a kill. Look at a door or a chalk drawing on a wall and press F to buy it. You can carry two weapons, and buying a third replaces the one in your hands. Buying from a gun's wall again refills its ammo for half price. Your pistol ammo refills every round. Co-op needs the downloadable version, because browsers can't open the network connection it uses.
